About

Meet Daniel Stofner

Founder of Ousia Design

I help clients transform ideas into spaces that are functional, timeless and deeply personal.

With a background in interior design and philosophy, I believe that great design starts with the right questions. It is not just about aesthetics — it is about understanding how people live, work and feel in a space.

My experience spans residential, hospitality and commercial projects in Italy and Switzerland, where I developed a holistic approach that bridges creativity, functionality, visualization and real-world execution.

Today, I work independently through Ousia Design and collaborate with private clients, studios and project partners on selected commissions and complementary project-based work.

My approach is not built around a fixed signature style. I look for the spatial logic, atmosphere and material language that belong to each project.

Background

Design practice informed by philosophy and execution.

Trained in interior design at the Florence Design Academy, I developed my practice through residential, hospitality and commercial projects, with a strong focus on concept development, visualization, custom furniture and technical design.

Working between Italy and Switzerland has given me experience across different project cultures, client expectations and implementation environments.

Alongside design, my studies in philosophy continue to shape how I think about perception, meaning, experience and the relationship between people and space.

That perspective supports a design process that is visually precise but never purely decorative: every intervention should have a reason.
