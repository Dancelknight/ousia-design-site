About

Interior design as a relationship between space, use and experience.

Ousia Design is an independent interior design practice focused on residential, hospitality and spatial concepts.

My work combines concept development, materiality, bespoke furniture and visual direction with a strong understanding of how design moves from idea to realization.

With a background in interior design and philosophy, I approach each project through both spatial clarity and a close attention to atmosphere, proportion and meaning.

Based in Italy and working internationally, I collaborate with private clients, design studios and project partners on selected commissions, project-based collaborations and remote design support.
