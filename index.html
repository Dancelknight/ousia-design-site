Collaboration in the canton of Zurich

Design and project management, together.

As an independent project manager and interior designer, I support architecture and interior design studios as well as clients in Zurich, Winterthur and across the canton. I join existing teams on a project basis – from concepts and technical development to coordination with specialist planners and contractors.

Space planning, materials, bespoke furniture, FF&E and 3D visualization are supported by structured project coordination. We agree on the scope and responsibilities to suit your project.

Based in Italy, I work remotely, with project meetings and on-site visits in the canton of Zurich by arrangement. When needed, I collaborate with Stofner Interior Design.
