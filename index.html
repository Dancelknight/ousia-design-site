A personal point of view.
A clear path forward.

Your home should express your personality through its layout, materials and details. Together, we develop a design direction that goes beyond familiar, off-the-shelf solutions.

From spatial concepts and material palettes to bespoke furniture and 3D visualisations, I make ideas tangible and decisions clear. Technical development and project coordination support the design within an agreed scope, working with your existing architects, specialists and contractors.

I am based in South Tyrol, where I returned with my family after living in Winterthur. My connection to Switzerland remains. I work on projects in Munich, Zurich and German-speaking Switzerland remotely and on site by arrangement. German is my native language; I also work in Italian and English.
