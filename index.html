Collaboration

Independent design capacity when a project needs it.

As a freelance interior designer, I support architecture and interior studios with space planning, material concepts, bespoke furniture and photorealistic 3D renderings. I also work with private clients and hospitality teams from concept through design development.

For larger executions, complex project phases and tasks that require broader implementation capacity, I work together with Stofner Interior Design, where I am a partner. This allows Ousia Design to remain personal and flexible while drawing on an established interior design structure when a project requires it.

Based in Italy, I offer remote collaboration for projects in Zurich, Winterthur, Bolzano, Munich, New York and beyond. On-site visits and interior project coordination can be arranged according to the scope and location.
