Profile

Daniel Stofner

Founder of Ousia Design

I help clients transform ideas into spaces that are functional, timeless and deeply personal.

With a background in interior design and philosophy, I believe strong design starts with the right questions — how people live, work and feel in a space.

I work independently through Ousia Design and collaborate with private clients, studios and project partners on selected commissions and project-based work.

I am also a partner at Stofner Interior Design. For larger executions and complex tasks, I work together with the Stofner team to connect independent design direction with established project delivery.

Background

Design practice informed by philosophy and execution.

Trained in interior design at the Florence Design Academy, I developed my practice through residential, hospitality and commercial projects, with a strong focus on concept development, visualization, custom furniture and technical design.

Alongside design, my studies in philosophy continue to shape how I think about perception, meaning, experience and the relationship between people and space.

Good design begins with the right questions.

Beyond design

Music & Art

Collaboration

Independent design capacity when a project needs it.

I collaborate with private clients, interior studios, architects, developers and hospitality teams on a flexible project basis.

For larger executions, complex project phases and tasks that require broader implementation capacity, I work together with Stofner Interior Design, where I am a partner. This allows Ousia Design to remain personal and flexible while drawing on an established interior design structure when a project requires it.

Support can range from concept development and spatial direction to visualization, custom furniture, design development and project coordination.
