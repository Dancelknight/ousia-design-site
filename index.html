Meet Daniel Stofner
Founder of Ousia Design

With a background in interior design and philosophy, I believe strong design starts with the right questions.

It is not just about aesthetics — it is about understanding how people live, work and feel in a space. My background combines residential, hospitality and commercial projects in Italy and Switzerland with project development, visualization, technical design and implementation.

Design is not only about how a space looks, but how it is understood, inhabited and remembered.

Ousia Design combines spatial thinking, material sensitivity and implementation knowledge to create interiors that are coherent, specific and lasting.

Available for selected commissions, complementary project-based collaboration, and remote studio support.

Collaboration

Independent design capacity when a project needs it.

I collaborate with private clients, interior studios, architects, developers and hospitality teams on a flexible project basis.

For larger executions, complex project phases and tasks that require broader implementation capacity, I work together with Stofner Interior Design, where I am a partner. This allows Ousia Design to remain personal and flexible while drawing on an established interior design structure when a project requires it.

Support can range from concept development and spatial direction to visualization, custom furniture, design development and project coordination.
